Identity and packaging for a line of creatine gummies.

Brand identity, packaging and visualization for MTRX. The system is built in English and Arabic and carries one geometric language from the wordmark through packaging, social and retail.

01

Brand

MTRX believes better isn’t given. It’s built through effort, intelligence, and intention. Progress takes more than hard work. It takes strategy and optimization. MTRX brings that mindset to supplementation with creatine gummies crafted to make smarter performance part of your routine.

02

Visual identity & wordmark

Bold typography, geometric patterns, and vibrant colors define a futuristic identity. The wordmark combines geometric letterforms with angular detailing to express precision and innovation, and its modular construction holds up across configurations and colors.

03

Graphic elements, color & type

The graphic elements extend the identity: the helix pattern duplicated into a repeating geometric pattern, techno elements to reinforce the futuristic theme, noise to break up swatches of color with texture, and blocked text for a brutalist feel.
