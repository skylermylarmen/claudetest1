The idea

Fire, taking different forms.

Fiamma is Italian for flame. The studio becomes a living light: it changes with the time of day, the class and the heat. It feels crafted and high-end, with a little feminine spark, and it's made for every body.

The Kit

A toolkit of light effects used everywhere: walls, socials, the website. These are the building blocks.

Ref 01
Kit · Glow + grain

The glow that shifts

The energy here belongs on social media. It's too loud for the studio itself, but two things go straight into the Kit.

  • A glow that drifts from hot red-orange toward pink
  • Analog grain over everything, so it feels warm and alive, never digital-flat
  • Fast one-word cuts for socials (HEAT, FLOW, BURN)

Type & Identity

Ref 04
Identity · Italian craft

Carved serifs & a crest

The name is Italian, so the type can be too. Beautiful carved serifs and an engraved crest give an elevated, versatile base.

  • High-end and crafted, with a real cultural touch point
  • Not the be-all and end-all. It's a direction to fuse with the atmosphere

The Space

From the moment you walk in, light leads the way. Concepts with buildout notes.

Ref 09
Space · Entry & hallway

Led in by light

The hallway stays dark. The only light comes from an LED strip that runs down the hall and pulls you into the main room.

  • Buildout: recessed LED channel along the ceiling or floor line
  • The strip can take the color of whatever class is running inside

A stone that glows

The front desk is a translucent stone that lights up from inside. It's the first thing you see, a warm welcome.
